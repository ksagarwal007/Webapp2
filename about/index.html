About Salarist

Last updated 12 Nov 2026

Salarist builds free decision tools for salaried people in India: when to take leave, whether to prepay a loan or invest, whether to rent or buy, and what your salary really becomes after tax.

What we promise

  • Plain answers. Each tool ends in a clear verdict, with the working shown.
  • Official numbers. Tax slabs, interest rates and holidays come from official sources, labelled with the tax year or date they apply to. How we check our numbers.
  • Privacy. Calculations run in your browser. We don't ask you to sign up and we don't store what you enter.

How Salarist is funded

The tools are free. We plan to show a small number of ads and some clearly labelled sponsored links, for example to travel or investment platforms. Sponsors never change what a tool calculates or recommends.

Not advice

Salarist gives estimates and comparisons to help you decide. It isn't tax, legal or financial advice. See our disclaimer.

Questions or corrections: contact us.
