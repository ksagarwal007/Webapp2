How we check our numbers

Official sources, dated and linked

Money decisions are only as good as the figures behind them, so we never work from memory or old articles.

  1. Official sources only

    Tax slabs from the Income-tax Act and Budget, savings rates from Ministry of Finance notices, holidays from DoPT and state orders.

  2. A date on every figure

    Each tool shows when we last verified it, and links to the document we checked.

  3. Updated when rules change

    After every Budget and every quarterly rate decision, we recheck the shared figures that all our tools use.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Salarist really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up. The site is supported by ads and clearly labelled sponsored links.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Calculations run in your browser. Your salary, loan and other figures are never sent to us or saved. Ad partners may set their own cookies, which our privacy policy explains.

Where do your tax rates and interest rates come from?

From official sources such as Ministry of Finance notifications, the Income-tax Act, 2025 and DoPT holiday orders. Each tool shows the date we last verified its figures and links to the source.

Is this financial advice?

No. Salarist gives you estimates and comparisons to help you decide. For decisions specific to your situation, speak to a qualified tax or financial adviser.

Does the leave planner work for private companies?

Yes. It starts from the central and state government holiday lists, and you can add or remove days to match your own company's holiday calendar.
