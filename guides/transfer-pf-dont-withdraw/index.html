Changed jobs? Transfer your PF, don't withdraw it

Changing jobs is the most common time to empty your PF account. It's also the most expensive. Here's what withdrawing really costs, and how to transfer your PF to your new employer instead.

By Salarist

Published 14 Nov 2026EPF Scheme 20263 min read

In this guide

  2. What withdrawing costs
  3. How to transfer your PF
  4. What happens to EPS
  5. Tax
  6. Check after the transfer

The short answer

Keep your UAN, and let your PF move to your new member ID: it often happens automatically, and otherwise takes one online request (Form 13). ₹3 lakh withdrawn at 28 would have been about ₹32.4 L at 58 at today's 8.25%. Withdrawing also resets your EPS service, and can make the money taxable.

See what your PF grows to

What withdrawing costs

  • Lost growth: ₹3 lakh left in EPF at 8.25% grows to about ₹32.4 L over 30 years, or ₹23.6 L over 26 years.
  • Your pension: if you also take the EPS withdrawal benefit, your EPS service starts from zero, and you may never reach the 10 years needed for a pension.
  • Tax: PF withdrawn before 5 years of continuous service is generally taxable.
  • Waiting: under the EPF Scheme, 2026, you can withdraw the full balance only after 12 months without a job, so withdrawing isn't quick anyway.

How to transfer your PF

  1. Give your new employer your existing UAN. Don't let them create a new one. They'll open a new member ID under it.
  2. Check that your UAN is linked to Aadhaar, and that your name and date of birth match. With these in place, EPFO usually starts the transfer automatically after your new employer's first contribution.
  3. If it doesn't start in a couple of months, file the transfer request (Form 13) on the EPFO member portal: choose the old member ID, and approve it with the OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  4. Make sure your old employer has marked your date of exit. If they haven't, you can update it yourself on the member portal once two months have passed since the last contribution.

What happens to EPS

Your EPS money doesn't move: it stays in the pension fund. What moves is your service record, so your years with the old employer keep counting towards the 10 years needed for a pension and towards its amount. Read EPS explained, or work out your pension with the EPS pension calculator.

Tax

After a transfer, your service with the old employer counts towards the 5 years of continuous service, so a later withdrawal can stay tax-free. Withdrawing instead of transferring breaks that count.

Check after the transfer

  • The old member ID's balance shows as zero and the new one shows it as "transfer in" in your passbook.
  • Your service history lists every employer with no gaps.
  • If your old employer ran its own PF trust, the PF comes from the trust and your EPS service from EPFO: check both.

Sources

Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026 and Employees' Pension Scheme, 2026 (notified 29 June 2026). EPFO member portal (transfer request, date of exit). Growth at 8.25%, the EPF rate approved for 2025-26, compounded yearly and assumed to stay the same. Read how we check our numbers.
