Best months for a week-long break in 2027

A week-long break usually costs five days of leave. In some months of 2027, holidays cut that to three. Here's the cheapest week-long break in every month of the year, so you can plan around the months that give you the most time off for your leave.

By Salarist

The short answer

For a 9-day break (two weekends and the week between), the best months in 2027 are March and May, which need only 3 days of leave. April gives the longest: 10 days for 4 days of leave. February, July, September, November, December have no weekday holidays to help, so a week off costs the full 5 days.

These are for a Monday-to-Friday week and the central government list. Your company's list may differ, so check yours in the planner.

The best months, explained

March: Holi and Good Friday

Holi falls on Tuesday 23 March and Good Friday on 26 March. Take leave on 22 Mar, 24 Mar, 25 Mar for Sat 20 Mar – Sun 28 Mar. If you get restricted holidays, Holika Dahan on Monday 22 March cuts it to 2 days: see which restricted holidays to pick.

April: Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti

Ram Navami is on Thursday 15 April and Mahavir Jayanti on Monday 19 April. Take leave on 12 Apr, 13 Apr, 14 Apr, 16 Apr for 10 days off, Sat 10 Apr – Mon 19 Apr.

May: Bakrid and Buddha Purnima

Id-ul-Zuha falls on Monday 17 May and Buddha Purnima on Thursday 20 May. Take leave on 18 May, 19 May, 21 May for Sat 15 May – Sun 23 May, a good time for the hills before the summer rush peaks.

Also good: January, June, August and October

Each needs 4 days of leave for 9 days off, around Republic Day, Muharram, Janmashtami and Diwali. The Diwali week (Sat 23 Oct – Sun 31 Oct) is the most popular, so apply early.

The hardest months

February, July, September, November, December need the full 5 days for a week off. Holidays in these months, like Guru Nanak's Birthday and Christmas, fall on a weekend in 2027. If you want a break then, a long weekend with one or two days of leave may be better value. The long weekends guide lists them all.

Before you book

  1. Check your company's 2027 holiday list and update the planner.
  2. Apply early for March, April and Diwali: everyone wants the same weeks.
  3. Book travel early too. Fares around these breaks rise as they approach.
  4. Some employers count weekends and holidays between leave days as leave (a sandwich rule). Check your policy.
  5. Plan the cost in your budget planner as a yearly travel line.

How these dates were worked out

Every break comes from the same calculation as the 2027 leave planner: for each month, every 9-day window starting in that month is checked, and the one needing the fewest leave days wins; days off on either side are added. It uses the central government list with a Monday-to-Friday week, from the DoPT office memorandum dated 16 July 2026. Read how we check our numbers.

Holiday dates for some festivals depend on the moon and may be changed by official notification. Always check your own employer's list before booking.
