How mutual fund gains are taxed when you sell

You pay tax on mutual funds only when you sell, and how much depends on the type of fund and how long you held it. Here are the rules for tax year 2026-27, what they mean for a SIP, and a worked example.

The short answer

Fund typeHeld 12 months or lessHeld over 12 months
Equity funds20%12.5% on gains above ₹1,25,000 a year
Debt funds (bought from 1 April 2023)Your slab rateYour slab rate

Add 4% cess to all of these. You pay nothing while you stay invested; tax is due in the year you sell.

See your SIP after tax

Equity funds

Funds that invest at least 65% in Indian shares are taxed as equity. If you sell units after more than 12 months, the gain is long-term: the first ₹1,25,000 of long-term gains in a tax year is tax-free, and the rest is taxed at 12.5% plus cess. If you sell within 12 months, the gain is short-term and taxed at 20% plus cess.

Debt funds

For debt fund units bought from 1 April 2023, the gain is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, however long you hold them. There's no lower long-term rate. That's why a debt fund's after-tax return is close to a fixed deposit's for people in the higher slabs.

How it works for a SIP

Each SIP instalment counts as a separate purchase with its own date. When you sell, the oldest units go first. So if you sell your whole SIP portfolio, the instalments from the last 12 months are short-term, and everything older is long-term.

An example

₹10,000 a month for 10 years, sold all at once at the end, all units treated as long-term:

  • Equity fund at 12% a year: it grows to ₹22.4 L on ₹12 L put in. Tax on the ₹10.4 L gain, after the ₹1,25,000 limit, is about ₹1,18,997, leaving ₹21.2 L.
  • Debt fund at 7% a year, 30% slab: it grows to ₹17.2 L. Tax on the ₹5.2 L gain is about ₹1,62,299, leaving ₹15.6 L.

Ways to pay less, legally

  1. Sell in stages. Spread equity withdrawals over two or three tax years to use the ₹1,25,000 limit each year.
  2. Book gains each year. Some investors sell and buy back enough units each year to realise ₹1,25,000 of long-term gain tax-free. Check exit loads first.
  3. Wait past 12 months before selling equity units, to pay 12.5% instead of 20%.
